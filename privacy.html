Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Finance is a personal, self-hosted app (about). Its only user is its developer. This policy describes how it handles Google user data.

Data accessed

  • Gmail (read-only): messages under a receipts label and their PDF attachments. Other mail is not read.
  • Google Sheets: the one grocery-sharing sheet the owner points the app at, to append rows (date, amount, receipt reference).

How it is used

Receipt attachments are parsed into items and totals so the owner can split shared groceries. The data is used for nothing else: no advertising, no profiling, no sale or transfer to anyone, and it is not used to train AI models.

Storage and sharing

Downloaded receipts, the parsed data and the Google sign-in token are stored only on the owner's own computers at home, reached over a private network. Encrypted backups of them stay under the owner's control. Nothing is shared with third parties, apart from the rows the owner deliberately writes to the shared sheet.

Limited Use

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The owner can delete the stored data by deleting the app's data folder.

Contact

Questions: the developer contact address listed on the app's Google consent screen.
